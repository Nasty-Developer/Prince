Long tail, each mapped to exactly one guide so nothing competes:

- what to do when you find an injured street dog → `/learn/what-to-do-when-you-find-an-injured-street-dog/`
- how to help stray dogs in India → `/learn/how-to-help-a-stray-dog-safely/`
- how to adopt an Indian street dog → `/learn/adopting-a-street-dog/`
- how to feed street dogs safely → `/learn/feeding-street-dogs-responsibly/`
- how to take care of stray puppies → `/learn/caring-for-a-rescue-puppy/`
- where to report an injured stray dog → `/learn/rescue-guidelines-and-when-to-call/`
- how to volunteer for animal rescue in India → `/#help`
- how to help abandoned puppies → `/learn/rescue-guidelines-and-when-to-call/`
- monsoon and summer safety for street dogs → `/learn/monsoon-and-summer-safety/`
- animal rights and community dogs → `/learn/animal-rights-and-responsible-ownership/`

Local keywords are deliberately thin, because no street address is published and service area is not confirmed. The only geographic signal used is the city already stated in existing site copy (Mumbai) plus `areaServed: India` in schema. Do not add localities until the team confirms where it actually works; invented service areas risk both ranking and credibility.

## 2. Pages optimised

Home, `/collar/`, `/stories/` and six story pages, `/learn/` and twelve guides: unique titles, descriptions, one H1, H2/H3 hierarchy, self-referencing canonicals, Open Graph and Twitter cards, `en-in` plus `x-default` hreflang, and JSON-LD (Organization, AnimalShelter, WebSite, WebPage, CollectionPage, Article, Product, BreadcrumbList).

## 3. Internal linking

Topic clusters, all links contextual in body copy:

- Home → ways to help, `/collar/`, `/#rescued`, `/stories/`, `/learn/`, `/#support`.
- Each guide → the two or three guides that continue the same reader journey, one relevant story, and `/#help` or `/collar/` where it fits the intent.
- Each story → related stories plus the guide that explains the practice behind it.
- Every page footer and nav reaches all sections, so nothing is orphaned more than one click from the home page.

## 4. Technical

- `sitemap.xml`: 22 canonical URLs, `lastmod`, priorities, and an image sitemap extension with 34 real image URLs.
- `robots.txt`: allows everything except `/admin`, declares the sitemap.
- Legacy routes 301-style meta refresh plus `noindex,follow`: `/adopt/`, `/puppies/`, `/products/`, `/checkout/`, `/orders/`, `/donate/`, `/report/`, `/admin/`, `/volunteer/`, `/foster/`, `/contact/`, and six retired guide URLs.
- No duplicate titles, descriptions or canonicals; no missing H1; no `noindex` on any canonical page; all images carry alt text.

## 5. Google Search Console steps (owner access required)

1. Open Search Console, add a **Domain** property for `savestreetdogss.com` (DNS verification at the registrar, covers www and apex), or a **URL-prefix** property for `https://savestreetdogss.com/` and verify with the HTML tag.
2. Submit `https://savestreetdogss.com/sitemap.xml`.
3. Request indexing for `/`, `/collar/`, `/stories/`, `/learn/` and the two highest-intent guides.
4. Under Settings → Crawl stats, confirm Googlebot fetches return 200. Nothing here blocks it.
5. Add Bing Webmaster Tools too; it imports the Search Console property.

## 6. Authority building, no spend

- Animal welfare and rescue directories: local NGO listings, shelter networks, volunteer matching platforms.
- Local press and rescue communities: offer the field notes as first-hand accounts, credited with a link.
- Educational partners: schools, vet colleges and colony-care groups linking to the first aid and ABC guides.
- Instagram and Facebook traffic is already linked from every page; post the guides as carousels with links in bio.
- Never buy links, never fabricate reviews or rescue outcomes, never use PBNs or automated link networks.

## 7. Measurement

Search Console queries and pages once indexed; no analytics scripts are installed, by design.
