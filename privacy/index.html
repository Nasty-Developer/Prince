Privacy policy

SaveStreet Dogs is a small street-dog rescue project in India. This page explains what happens to information when you read the site, order a collar or send a donation. It is short, because the site collects very little.

Last updated 8 November 2026

What this website is

This is a static website. Every page is a file, not an application that keeps a record of you. There are no accounts, no logins, no comment threads and no database of visitors. Nothing you do here creates a profile that we can look at later.

What we do not collect

There is no analytics script on this site, no advertising pixel, no heat-map recorder and no newsletter signup. We do not set marketing cookies. We do not buy or rent contact lists. If you never message us, we never learn that you visited.

The collar order form

The order form on the shop page runs entirely in your browser. It takes the size, colour, quantity, name, mobile number and address you type, assembles them into a message, and opens WhatsApp with that message ready to send.

Nothing leaves your device until you press send inside WhatsApp. The site itself has no server that receives the form, so we cannot see an order you abandon halfway. Once you do send it, the message arrives on the SaveStreet Dogs WhatsApp number, +91 89554 38209, and is handled there under WhatsApp's own privacy terms. We use it to confirm the order, calculate delivery and stay in touch about that order. We do not add those numbers to a mailing list.

Donations

The amount buttons and the QR code open your own UPI application with the payment details already filled in: UPI ID 7413073410@ptaxis, payee Pawan Vaishnav. The payment, and the record of it, stays between you, your bank and that UPI account. We never receive your card number, bank credentials or UPI PIN, and this site stores none of them. Your UPI app shows you exactly what is shared with the payee before you approve it.

Storage on your device

The site stores one value in your browser's session storage, a small flag that keeps the opening animation from replaying on every page you open in the same visit. It closes when you close the tab. No cookies are set, and nothing is written to local storage. Clearing site data in your browser removes it.

Hosting and server logs

Pages are served through Render and Cloudflare. Like any hosting service, they may keep technical records such as the IP address that requested a page, the browser and operating system, the URL, and the time of the request. Those records exist for delivering the site and protecting it from abuse. We do not use them to identify individual visitors, and we cannot switch them off from inside the site.

Links to other services

This site links out to WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook. Those are separate services with their own privacy policies. No Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp widgets are embedded in these pages, so simply reading a page here does not load anything from them. Once you follow a link, their rules apply rather than ours.

Photographs

The photographs on this site show street dogs we have reached. We do not run facial recognition, and we do not collect information about visitors from images. If a photograph shows you and you would prefer it not to, message us and tell us which page it is on.

Children

The site is written for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from a child. If a child has sent us a message and you would like it removed, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can read every page here without sending us anything. You can close the order form, or the WhatsApp window, without replying. If you have already messaged us and want that conversation deleted, ask and we will remove it from the phone it was received on. Because we hold no database beyond those messages, most requests are answered the same day.

Getting in touch

Questions about this policy, or about information you have already sent us, can go to the same two places we answer everything else: WhatsApp on +91 89554 38209, or a direct message on Instagram.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts doing something new that involves information, this page will say so before it happens, and the date at the top will change. There is no mailing list to notify, so the page itself is the notice.
