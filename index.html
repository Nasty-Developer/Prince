Real photos

Most get out. Not all.

Every photo here is a dog we reached: puppies picked up at night, a wound cleaned where the dog was lying, a drip line on a tiled floor. Most dogs we find do come through. Some we cannot, because they are too fast, too sick, or already gone by the time the call comes. We show the real ones instead of only the happy endings, because the next dog depends on people knowing what this actually looks like.

More photos are added as they come in. Names and dates are left off on purpose: many of these dogs are still on the street, and the team protects where they are.
